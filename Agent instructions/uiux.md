# UI/UX Design Specification

# Gifts of Printing Website

**Version:** 1.0
**Project Type:** Premium Business Website
**Design Platform:** Google Stitch (MCP)
**Development Platform:** Google Antigravity
**Frontend:** React 19 + Vite

---

# 1. Design Vision

The Gifts of Printing website should communicate creativity, trust, professionalism, and premium craftsmanship through a modern, minimal, and visually engaging interface.

The overall experience should feel similar to brands like Apple, Stripe, Framer, and Linear, combining elegant typography, generous white space, premium product imagery, subtle animations, and intuitive navigation.

The design should inspire confidence while encouraging visitors to request quotations and explore personalized printing services.

---

# 2. Design Principles

The interface must follow these core principles:

* Simplicity over complexity
* Premium visual quality
* User-first navigation
* Consistent design language
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Mobile-first experience
* Fast interaction
* Clear calls-to-action
* Accessibility by default
* Responsive across all devices

Every section should have a clear purpose without visual clutter.

# 33. Success Metrics

The UI/UX will be considered successful when:

* Users understand the business within the first few seconds.
* Navigation is intuitive on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Product categories and services are easy to discover.
* Portfolio images attract attention without slowing the site.
* Contact methods are easy to find.
* Primary CTAs consistently encourage quote requests.
* The design maintains a premium, cohesive appearance across every page.
* Accessibility standards are met while preserving visual elegance.
* Visitors experience smooth interactions with minimal friction from entry to enquiry.
